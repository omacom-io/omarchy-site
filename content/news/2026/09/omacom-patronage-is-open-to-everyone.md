The reception of the Omacom Foundation has been nothing short of amazing. Twelve Founding Patrons, our first two Distinguished Corporate Patrons, and $12.6 million committed to the mission in under two weeks. But the foundation is not just for [the Oligarchy](https://oligarchy.fyi) and the Corporate Patrons. It's for everyone. Anyone who can get behind beautiful, fun & agentic Linux.

So as of today, [donations are open to patrons of all means](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/omarchy-patronage). Five bucks, fifty bucks, whatever fits. Every patron is part of the same mission, and every dollar buys the same thing: open-source developers getting paid to build the future of the Linux desktop.

And we intend to announce more spending very soon! A new $10,000 plugin competition with more winners than the first one. More big grants to the projects and people Omarchy depends on.

Because this money is not being raised to start the Iron Bank. We're aiming to spend everything over five years. More patrons, more spending. Everyone can be part of this.

[Become a patron today](https://www.zeffy.com/en-US/donation-form/omarchy-patronage).
