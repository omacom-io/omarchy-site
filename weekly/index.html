Omarchy Weekly

Omarchy newsletter

A concise weekly digest of Omarchy Linux news, tips, workflow ideas, community projects, and release notes.

Get the next issue in your inbox. One email a week, and nothing else.

What’s in the newsletter

Issues aimed to be short enough to read with a coffee, and collects a curated list of news and changes in the world of Omarchy:

  • News from Omarchy core
  • Articles and screencasts of tips and workflow ideas for getting more out of Omarchy
  • Community projects: themes, plugins, scripts, and dotfiles worth a look
  • Omarchy events
  • New releases and notable pull requests merged to Omarchy
